OvenTiny is an independent Toronto streetwear and art brand built around original, hand-drawn characters. We believe clothing can be a canvas, creativity should stay playful, and the best ideas often come from unlikely collaborations.

From limited-run apparel to projects with local businesses, everything starts with a sketch and a little curiosity.
